The line is never answered by a server. It connects first, and the platform answers only when the child picks up.

Issuing the connect on an unanswered leg acknowledges the offer (one offer instead of nineteen) and defers billing to the bridge. Each scenario below places a real call through the whitelist and shows the signaling and the money as they happen.

PROMISE P1
Spam costs nothing

Billing starts at answer. Unanswered and declined calls show billing_ms: null — zero minutes, every leg.

PROMISE P2
Blocked callers learn nothing

A declined caller's phone never shows connected. They get a busy-style failure and no timer ever starts.

PROMISE P3
Answered means the child answered

The A-leg flips to answered at the child's pickup, so the call record reflects a real conversation.
